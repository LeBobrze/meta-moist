<p>The <strong>fish button</strong> is a button that theoretically could have been added to the Nintendo Switch 2, and would do nothing when pressed. It originates from a meme mimicing a real post about a Switch 2 leak, which correctly claimed that the Switch 2 would not feature a fish button that when pressed does nothing.</p>
