<p><strong>Fan Art</strong> is a channel in the [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums Discord server]] for posting and discussing [[DashNet]] related fan art. Art posted in the channel is often reacted to with spaghetti emojis, indicating that the art is "delicious" in a metaphorical sense. Any art that is formatted properly and took some effort are reposted in the Gallery channel, where all finished fan art can be enjoyed without the discussion and development surrounding it. The channel is visible to all Awakened users, but only users with the "Art fan" role can send messages.</p>
