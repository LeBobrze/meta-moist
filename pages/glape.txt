<p>glape is a custom emote commonly used my [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]], and less commonly by other members of [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]]. It is used to represent a feeling of being scared, as the emote features a scared cat. It is also occasionally remixed into other emotes, such as "grape", playing on the rhyme with glape.
