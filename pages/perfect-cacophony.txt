<p>Perfect Cacophony is a difficult custom night from the Five Nights at Freddy's fangame "Sister Location Super Custom Night". The night features many animatronic threats, such as [[bidybab|Bidybab]], which you must survive for a total of 6 minutes. It is currently ranked at #17 on the All Modes List. [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]] is currently trying to complete it.
