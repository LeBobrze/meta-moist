<p><strong>XP roles</strong> are roles in the [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums Discord server]] that can be attained by gaining a certain amount of Server XP. There are 12 XP roles, gained at different amounts of XP. Each one has a name related to Cookie Clicker. Some XP roles grant extra permissions in the server.</p>
