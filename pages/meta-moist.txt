<p>Meta Moist refers to the collection of all things and concepts related to the [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums Discord server]] and/or its community. It can also be used to refer to things that are part of Meta Moist, or as an adjective to describe things that are part of Meta Moist. It is named after [[meta-moist-thread|the Meta Moist thread]], which is dedicated to the discussion of Meta Moist.</p>
<p>The Meta Moist canon is the context in which all Meta Moist jokes are true.</p>
