<p><strong>The Game of Mao</strong>, often abbreviated as <strong>GoM</strong> or shortened to the noun <strong>gom</strong>, is a message-based rule-guessing game that is played in a post in the Other Games channel of [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]]. The main gameplay is that there is a secret rule that applies to all messages sent in the thread. After each message, someone who knows the secret rule (nicknamed a gommod, short for GoM moderator) will "grade" the message by reacting with a thumbs up or thumbs down emoji to indicate whether the message follows or breaks the secret rule. The sender of a message can declare if they think their message will break the rule, and otherwise it is assumed they think their message will follow the rule. If their declaration matches the actual grade, the player can continue to send new messages, otherwise their messages are no longer graded for 12 hours. Once a player knows what the secret rule is, they may guess. If they correctly guess the rule, the rule ends and the player may devise the next rule.</p>
<p>The post is tagged "Puzzle", "Role-playing", and "Card game". The "Card game" tag is due to the game's relation to the card game known as Mao, which involves a secret set of rules that new players must figure out based on the response to their actions. Different versions of the text-based GoM can be found in other forums.</p>
<p>The DashNet Forums instance of GoM was started by Trufflz in September of 2023, making Trufflz a permanent gommod, sometimes nicknamed a "gomlord". Trufflz created all rules until April 2024. Afterwards, each rule solver was allowed to create the next rule. After Trufflz became less active, the community appointed other gomlords as replacements.</p>
<p>After The Game of Mao became popular, many variants were devised by other members and played in new posts in the Other Games channel. Some examples include The Game of Mao II, where two rules are active simultaneously and guessed independently, and The Game of Gom, where each rule is a different rule-guessing game in and of itself.</p>
