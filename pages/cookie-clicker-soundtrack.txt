<p><strong><cite>Cookie Clicker</cite></strong> is the soundtrack for the Steam version of [[cookie-clicker|Cookie Clicker]]. It was created by C418 and released in 2021. It is 22 minutes and 16 seconds long with only 5 tracks, making each track about 4:27 on average.</p>
