<p>Bidybab is one of the main threats featured in [[perfect-cacophony|Perfect Cacophony]]. She attacks through the top vent, and you must watch her on the camera with the door closed to push her back and prevent her from killing you. She is often found to be one of the hardest animatronics to deal with throughout the night, due to having to constantly check on her because of her fast movement speed. This difficulty is what caused her to feature in the [[perfect-cacophony-meme|Perfect Cacophony meme]], made by [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]].
