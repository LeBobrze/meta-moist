<p>The hammers are coming is a meme that is commonly referenced in [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]] by legend6667585 and [[foxgirlmaki|Maki]]. It was originally made and posted by legend6667585.</p><p>The meme is derived from a similar, more popular meme captioned "Bitches be like: 'I have my whole life ahead of me'. No you don't, the fog is coming". In the meme, "life" is replaced by a screenshot of DashNet Forums, and "fog" is replaced by a frame from a GIF of a cat being repeatedly tapped on the head with a hammer. This GIF has been posted in DashNet Forums as early as 2024. The meme also excludes the phrase "Bitches be like".</p>
