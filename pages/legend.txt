<p><strong>Legend</strong> is a member of [[dashnet-forums-discord-server|DashNet Forums]]. Usually seen in the server with the nickname "seventeen leignd", formerly "eleven leignd", and is often referred to simply as "leignd". Legend holds the [[cookie-clicker|Cookie Clicker]] record for most reindeers collected in the competitive ruleset. His current reindeer count is appended to the end of his nickname. In a Jackbox party game among other members of DashNet Forums, Legend once shortened his name to "leg6667585", causing some members in [[dashnets-best-gamers|Dashnet's Best Gamers]] to add "leg" to their name. This naming scheme was mildly enforced by [[Fractyl]], who manually changed the nicknames of several users to include "leg".</p>
